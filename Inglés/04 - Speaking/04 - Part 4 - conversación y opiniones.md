<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">En la Parte 4 el examinador amplía el tema de la Parte 3 con preguntas más generales de opinión</mark> (p. ej. si la tarea era sobre regalos: "Do you think it's better to give or receive presents? Why?"). Ahora hablas de ti y de tu visión, no de decidir.

# Qué se espera

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Dar tu **opinión**, justificarla con **razones** y apoyarla con **ejemplos o experiencias**</mark>. Y, como en la Parte 3, reaccionar a lo que diga tu pareja.

# El molde de una buena respuesta

**Opinión → razón → ejemplo/experiencia:**

*"In my opinion, it's better to give presents **because** it makes you feel good. **For example**, last year I gave my mum a surprise and her face was priceless."*

# Frases para opinar y matizar

- **Opinar:** *In my opinion… / I think… / If you ask me… / Personally, I feel…*
- **Generalizar:** *Most people… / In general… / It depends on…*
- **Matizar:** *It depends. On one hand… on the other hand…*
- **Reaccionar a la pareja:** *I agree with X. That's a good point. I see it differently.* (para referir lo que dijo, ver [[03 - Reported speech (estilo indirecto)]]).

> [!warning]+ Trampas ("para pillar")
> - Responder **sí/no** sin justificar.
> - No conectar con la pareja (*Do you agree, …?*).
> - Repetir siempre *"I think"*: varía con *In my opinion, Personally, If you ask me*.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Da una respuesta completa (opinión + razón + ejemplo)
> "Do you think people spend too much money on presents?"

> [!success]- Solución 1
> Ejemplo: *"In my opinion, yes, sometimes they do, because they feel pressured to impress. For example, at Christmas many people buy expensive gifts they can't really afford."*

> [!question]+ 2. Reformula "I think" con otra fórmula de opinión (x2)
> "I think sport is important." → ______ / ______

> [!success]- Solución 2
> *In my opinion, sport is important.* / *Personally, I feel sport is important.* / *If you ask me, sport is important.*

> [!question]+ 3. Matiza con "it depends"
> "Is it better to live in a city or a village?" → empieza con "It depends…"

> [!success]- Solución 3
> Ejemplo: *"It depends. On one hand, cities have more opportunities; on the other hand, villages are quieter and cheaper."*
