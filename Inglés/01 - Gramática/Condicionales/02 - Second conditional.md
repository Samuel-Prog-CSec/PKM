<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El *second conditional* habla de situaciones **hipotéticas, imaginarias o improbables** en el presente o el futuro</mark>.

# Estructura

**If + past simple, + would + verbo base.**

*If I **won** the lottery, I**'d travel** the world. If I **had** more time, I**'d learn** the piano.*

El past simple aquí **no es pasado**: expresa irrealidad. En la principal, además de `would`, caben `could` y `might`: *If I studied more, I **could** pass.*

# If I were you (consejo)

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Con el verbo *to be*, se usa `were` para todas las personas</mark> en el condicional hipotético: *If I **were** you, I'd apologise. If he **were** rich…* (aunque *was* se oye en habla informal, en examen usa **were**). Es la fórmula estrella para **dar consejos**.

# First vs second

- **First** (real, probable): *If I **have** time, I**'ll call** you* (es posible que tenga tiempo).
- **Second** (hipotético, improbable): *If I **had** time, I**'d call** you* (pero no lo tengo).

# Ampliación: third conditional (pasado imaginario)

Para algo **hipotético en el pasado** (que ya no se puede cambiar) existe el *third conditional*: **If + past perfect, + would have + participio**. *If I **had studied**, I **would have passed*** (pero no estudié). Es B2, pero cae en reading.

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `would` tras `if`: *If I would have money* ❌ → *If I **had** money* ✅.
> - Usar *was* en el consejo formal: en examen, *If I **were** you*.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **El past simple del second conditional NO es pasado**: es hipótesis presente/futura.
> - **`If I were you`** con *were*, no *was*.
> - Real → first; imaginario/improbable → second; pasado imaginario → third.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena (second conditional)
> 1. If I ______ (be) you, I ______ (not/do) that.
> 2. If we ______ (have) a car, we ______ (travel) more.
> 3. She ______ (be) happier if she ______ (change) jobs.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **were / wouldn't do** — 2. **had / would travel** — 3. **would be / changed**

> [!question]+ 2. ¿first o second? Elige
> 1. If I ______ rich, I'd buy a boat. → (a) am (b) were
> 2. If it ______ later, I'll wait for you. → (a) is (b) were

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **b** (hipotético) — 2. **a** (real → first)

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. If I would win, I'd be happy.
> 2. If he was taller, he could be a model. (formal)
> 3. If she studies harder, she would pass. (mezcla de tiempos)

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. If I **won**, I'd be happy — 2. If he **were** taller… — 3. If she **studied** harder, she would pass (o *studies… will pass* para first)

> [!question]+ 4. Traduce (usa If I were you)
> 1. Yo que tú, descansaría.
> 2. Si tuviera dinero, viajaría.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **If I were you, I'd rest / I would rest.** — 2. **If I had money, I would travel.**
