<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Las conjunciones temporales (`when, as soon as, until, before, after, while`) que hablan del futuro llevan **presente**, no `will`</mark>. Funcionan como el *if* del first conditional.

# Estructura

**Conjunción + present simple … , … will + base** (y viceversa).

*I**'ll call** you when I **arrive**. As soon as she **gets** home, she**'ll ring** you. We won't start until everyone **is** here.*

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Tras estas conjunciones, futuro = presente</mark>: *when I will arrive* ❌ → *when I **arrive*** ✅.

# Matices de cada una

- **`when`** = cuando (seguro que pasa). **`if`** = si (puede que pase): *When I see her* (la veré) vs *If I see her* (quizá).
- **`as soon as`** = en cuanto (inmediatez).
- **`until`** = hasta que (+ acción que dura): *Wait here until I **come** back.*
- **`before / after`**: *Finish before you **leave**. We'll eat after we **arrive**.*

# Casos atípicos

- Para marcar anterioridad se puede usar **present perfect** tras la conjunción: *I'll call you when I**'ve finished**.* (= una vez haya acabado).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `will` tras la conjunción temporal: *After I will eat* ❌ → *After I **eat*** ✅.
> - Confundir `when` (seguro) con `if` (condición).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`when/as soon as/until/before/after/while` + presente** para el futuro; `will` solo en la principal.
> - `until` marca el límite temporal; no confundir con `by` (fecha tope).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena (presente o will)
> 1. I ______ (phone) you as soon as I ______ (know) the results.
> 2. Wait here until the rain ______ (stop).
> 3. When you ______ (finish), we ______ (go) out.
> 4. She'll read the book before she ______ (see) the film.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **'ll phone / know** — 2. **stops** — 3. **finish / 'll go** — 4. **sees**

> [!question]+ 2. when o if
> 1. ______ I get home tonight (seguro), I'll cook dinner.
> 2. ______ you need help (quizá), call me.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **When** — 2. **If**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. I'll tell her when I will see her.
> 2. We won't leave until it will stop raining.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. …when I **see** her — 2. …until it **stops** raining

> [!question]+ 4. Une las frases
> 1. I / get / paid + I / buy / you dinner (as soon as)
> 2. Don't go out + the rain / stop (until)

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. *As soon as I **get** paid, I**'ll buy** you dinner.* — 2. *Don't go out until the rain **stops**.*
