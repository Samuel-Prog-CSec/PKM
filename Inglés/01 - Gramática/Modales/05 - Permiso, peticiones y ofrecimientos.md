Estos usos son puro **speaking y writing** funcional: pedir cosas con educación. El modal elegido marca el **registro**.

# Permiso

- **Pedir:** `Can I…?` (informal), `Could I…?` (más educado), `May I…?` (formal). *__May I__ come in?*
- **Dar / denegar:** *You **can** go. / You **can't** park here.*

# Peticiones (pedir a otro que haga algo)

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">`Can/Could/Would you…?` piden a alguien que haga algo</mark>, de menos a más formal: *__Can__ you pass the salt? __Could__ you help me? __Would__ you mind closing the door?*

`Would you mind + -ing` es muy educado, y su respuesta es contraintuitiva: <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">"No" significa "adelante"</mark> (no me molesta).

# Ofrecimientos y sugerencias

- **Ofrecer ayuda:** `Shall I…?` / `Can I…?` — *__Shall I__ carry that for you?*
- **Sugerir hacer algo juntos:** `Shall we…?` / `Why don't we…?` / `Let's…` — *__Shall we__ start?*
- **Ofrecer algo:** `Would you like…?` — *__Would you like__ a coffee?* (no *Do you want*, que suena brusco).

| Registro | Permiso | Petición |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Informal | Can I? | Can you? |
| Neutro/educado | Could I? | Could you? |
| Formal | May I? | Would you…? |

# Casos atípicos

- **`Would you mind if I…?`** + past simple (no presente): *Would you mind **if I opened** the window?*
- **`I'd like`** (= quiero, educado) frente a **`Do you like?`** (= te gusta): no confundir *I'd like a coffee* (quiero) con *I like coffee* (me gusta).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Responder mal a *Would you mind…?*: si accedes, di *No, not at all / Of course not* (no *Yes*).
> - *Do you want…?* para ofrecer: correcto pero brusco; en examen usa *Would you like…?*.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`Would you mind + -ing`** (no infinitivo): *Would you mind **to close**?* ❌ → *…mind **closing**?* ✅.
> - **`Would you mind if I` + past simple**: *…if I **sat** here?*
> - **`Shall I/we`** para ofrecimientos y sugerencias en 1ª persona.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena con el modal adecuado
> 1. ______ I use your phone, please? (formal)
> 2. ______ you like something to drink?
> 3. It's hot in here. ______ I open the window? (ofrecimiento)
> 4. ______ we go to the cinema tonight? (sugerencia)

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **May** — 2. **Would** — 3. **Shall** — 4. **Shall** (o *Why don't*)

> [!question]+ 2. Elige la opción correcta
> 1. Would you mind ______ the door? → (a) to close (b) closing
> 2. Would you mind if I ______ here? → (a) sit (b) sat
> 3. ______ a sandwich? → (a) Do you like (b) Would you like

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **b** — 2. **b** — 3. **b**

> [!question]+ 3. "Would you mind opening the window?" Quieres acceder. ¿Qué respondes?
> (a) Yes, of course. (b) No, not at all.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> **(b)** No, not at all (acceder = "no me molesta").

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el error
> 1. Would you mind to help me?
> 2. Do you want a coffee? (hazlo más educado)
> 3. Shall we to start?

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. …mind **helping** me? — 2. **Would you like** a coffee? — 3. Shall we **start**?
