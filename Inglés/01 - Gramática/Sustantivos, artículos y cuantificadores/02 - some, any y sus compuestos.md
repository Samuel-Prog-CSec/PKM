<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">`some` y `any` expresan una cantidad indefinida</mark> con incontables y contables en plural.

# La regla básica

| | some | any |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | ✔ *I have **some** money.* | — |
| Negativa | — | ✔ *I don't have **any** money.* |
| Pregunta | (ofrecimiento/petición) | ✔ *Do you have **any** money?* |

# Las dos excepciones de some en preguntas

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">`some` sí aparece en preguntas cuando ofreces o pides algo</mark>, esperando un "sí":

- **Ofrecimiento:** *Would you like **some** coffee?*
- **Petición:** *Can I have **some** water?*

# any con sentido de "cualquiera"

En frases afirmativas, `any` significa **"cualquiera"**: *You can take **any** seat. **Anyone** can do it.*

# Compuestos

`some/any/no/every` + `-thing / -body(-one) / -where`:

| | cosa | persona | lugar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| some- | something | somebody/someone | somewhere |
| any- | anything | anybody/anyone | anywhere |
| no- | nothing | nobody/no one | nowhere |
| every- | everything | everybody/everyone | everywhere |

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Los compuestos con `no-` ya son negativos: el verbo va en **afirmativa**</mark>: *I saw **nobody*** (= I didn't see anybody). *I didn't see nobody* ❌ (doble negación).

Los compuestos con *-body/-one/-thing* llevan **verbo en singular**: *Everybody **is** here. Nothing **works**.*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `some` en negativas: *I don't have some* ❌ → *…**any*** ✅.
> - Doble negación: *I don't know nothing* ❌ → *I **don't** know **anything*** / *I know **nothing*** ✅.
> - Verbo en plural con *everybody*: *Everybody are* ❌ → *Everybody **is*** ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **Ofrecimiento/petición → `some`** aunque sea pregunta.
> - **`no-` = afirmativa** (una sola negación).
> - **Compuestos + verbo singular**.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. some o any
> 1. There's ______ milk in the fridge.
> 2. I don't need ______ help.
> 3. Would you like ______ tea?
> 4. Is there ______ sugar left?

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **some** — 2. **any** — 3. **some** — 4. **any**

> [!question]+ 2. Completa con un compuesto (something, anywhere, nobody...)
> 1. I'm hungry. I want ______ to eat.
> 2. ______ answered the phone; the office was empty.
> 3. Did you go ______ nice at the weekend?

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **something** — 2. **Nobody / No one** — 3. **anywhere**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error (doble negación / concordancia)
> 1. I didn't see nothing.
> 2. Everybody are welcome.
> 3. Nobody don't likes him.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. I didn't see **anything** (o *I saw nothing*) — 2. Everybody **is** welcome — 3. Nobody **likes** him

> [!question]+ 4. ¿Cuál es la frase CORRECTA?
> 1. (a) Can I have any water? (b) Can I have some water? (petición)
> 2. (a) There isn't nobody here. (b) There's nobody here.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **b** — 2. **b**
