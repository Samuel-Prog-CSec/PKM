<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Los cuantificadores dicen "cuánto/cuántos", y elegir bien depende de si el sustantivo es contable o incontable</mark>.

# mucho / muchos

| | Contables (plural) | Incontables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| en negativas/preguntas | **many** (many books) | **much** (much time) |
| en cualquier frase | **a lot of / lots of** | **a lot of / lots of** |

*How **many** eggs? I don't have **much** money. There are **a lot of** people.*

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">`much` y `many` suenan formales en afirmativa</mark>: se prefiere `a lot of` (*I have a lot of friends*, no *many friends* en habla neutra).

# poco / pocos

| | Contables | Incontables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| poco (escaso, negativo) | **few** | **little** |
| un poco (algo, positivo) | **a few** | **a little** |

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">`few/little` (sin *a*) = casi nada, tono negativo; `a few/a little` = algo, tono positivo</mark>: *I have **a few** friends* (algunos) vs *I have **few** friends* (casi ninguno, me siento solo).

# demasiado y suficiente

- **`too much / too many`** = demasiado (exceso): *too much salt, too many cars.*
- **`enough`** = suficiente; va **antes del sustantivo** pero **después del adjetivo**: *enough money* / *old **enough*** (a fondo en [[03 - too, enough, so, such]]).
- **`too` + adjetivo** = demasiado: *too expensive*.

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **`too much` ≠ `very much`**: *too much* es negativo (exceso); *very much* es intensidad (*Thank you very much*).
- **`How much…?`** también pregunta el precio: *How much is it?*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `much` con contables: *much people* ❌ → *many/a lot of people* ✅.
> - `enough` mal colocado: *enough big* ❌ → *big **enough*** ✅.
> - Confundir `few` (negativo) con `a few` (positivo).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`many`/`few`/`a few` → contables**; **`much`/`little`/`a little` → incontables**.
> - **`enough`**: antes del nombre, después del adjetivo.
> - **`few` vs `a few`** cambia el tono (casi ninguno vs algunos).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Elige el cuantificador (much/many/a lot of)
> 1. How ______ money do you need?
> 2. There aren't ______ chairs.
> 3. She has ______ books (afirmativa neutra).

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **much** — 2. **many** — 3. **a lot of**

> [!question]+ 2. few, a few, little o a little
> 1. I have ______ time, so let's be quick. (casi nada)
> 2. Don't worry, we have ______ minutes left. (algunos)
> 3. There's ______ hope. (positivo: algo)
> 4. Very ______ people came; it was empty. (casi nadie)

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **little** — 2. **a few** — 3. **a little** — 4. **few**

> [!question]+ 3. Coloca enough
> 1. He isn't (old / enough) to drive.
> 2. We don't have (enough / money).

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. old **enough** — 2. **enough** money

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el error
> 1. There are too much cars in the city.
> 2. She has a few money.
> 3. The coffee isn't enough hot.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. too **many** cars — 2. a **little** money — 3. …isn't hot **enough**
