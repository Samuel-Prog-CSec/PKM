<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Un sustantivo **contable** se puede numerar (one book, two books); uno **incontable** no (agua, dinero, información)</mark>. Esta distinción decide qué artículo y qué cuantificador puedes usar.

# Cómo se comportan

| | Contable | Incontable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plural | sí (books) | no (*informations* ❌) |
| Verbo | singular o plural | siempre **singular** |
| a/an | sí (a book) | no (*a water* ❌) |
| some/any | sí | sí |
| much / many | **many** | **much** |

*The **news is** good. This **information is** useful.* (verbo en singular).

# Incontables que despistan (para pillar)

<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Muchos sustantivos que en español son contables, en inglés son incontables</mark> y no tienen plural ni `a/an`:

`information, advice, furniture, luggage, news, money, bread, work, homework, research, traffic, weather, accommodation, progress, knowledge`.

*an advice* ❌ → *a piece of advice* ✅ · *furnitures* ❌ → *furniture* ✅.

# Cómo "contar" un incontable

Con un **partitivo**: *a piece of advice, a bit of information, a slice of bread, a bottle of water, a loaf of bread, two kilos of rice.*

# Casos atípicos

- Algunos sustantivos son **ambos** con significado distinto: *a coffee* (una taza) vs *coffee* (la sustancia); *a paper* (un periódico) vs *paper* (papel); *hair* (pelo, incontable) vs *a hair* (un pelo suelto).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Pluralizar incontables: *informations, advices, furnitures* ❌.
> - Verbo en plural con incontables: *The news are* ❌ → *The news **is*** ✅.
> - *a/an* con incontables: *a music* ❌.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`people` es plural** (verbo en plural) y **`person`** su singular: *People **are** nice.*
> - **`money, information, advice, furniture` = singular incontable**.
> - Para **much/many, a lot of, (a) few / (a) little**, ver [[03 - Cuantificadores de cantidad]].

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. ¿Contable (C) o incontable (I)?
> money · book · advice · chair · furniture · idea · water · news

> [!success]- Solución 1
> money **I** · book **C** · advice **I** · chair **C** · furniture **I** · idea **C** · water **I** · news **I**

> [!question]+ 2. Corrige el error
> 1. She gave me a good advice.
> 2. The furnitures are new.
> 3. The news are bad today.
> 4. I need some informations.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. …a **piece of advice** — 2. The **furniture is** new — 3. The news **is** bad — 4. …some **information**

> [!question]+ 3. Elige much o many
> 1. There isn't ______ milk. 2. How ______ people came? 3. I don't have ______ time.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **much** — 2. **many** — 3. **much**

> [!question]+ 4. Añade un partitivo
> 1. a ______ of advice — 2. a ______ of bread — 3. a ______ of water

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **piece** — 2. **slice/loaf** — 3. **bottle/glass**
