<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El *past perfect* es "el pasado antes del pasado": marca que una acción ocurrió **antes** que otra acción pasada</mark>. Ordena dos hechos en el tiempo.

# Estructura

**had + participio pasado** (igual para todas las personas; contracción `'d`). *I **had finished** before she arrived. They **hadn't left** yet. **Had** you **seen** it?*

# Cuándo se usa

- **Anterioridad entre dos pasados:** *When we **got** to the station, the train **had** already **left**.* (primero se fue el tren; luego llegamos).
- Con `after, before, by the time, already, just, never...before`: *By the time she called, I **had gone** to bed.*
- En **estilo indirecto** (el past simple/present perfect del original pasa a past perfect): *"I saw him" → She said she **had seen** him* (ver [[03 - Reported speech (estilo indirecto)]]).
- En el **tercer condicional** (ver [[02 - Second conditional]]): *If I **had known**, I would have helped.*

# Cuándo NO hace falta

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Si el orden ya está claro por `before`/`after`, el past perfect es opcional</mark>: *After I **finished** / **had finished**, I left.* Se reserva para cuando el orden podría confundir o para resaltar la anterioridad.

# Past perfect simple vs continuous

Igual que en presente, existe el **past perfect continuous** (*had been + -ing*) para la duración previa a otro pasado: *She was tired because she **had been working** all night.*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Usar past simple donde hace falta marcar anterioridad clara: *When I arrived, he left* (ambiguo/secuencia) vs *When I arrived, he **had left*** (ya se había ido).
> - Poner past perfect en las dos acciones: solo la **anterior** lo lleva; la posterior va en past simple.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`By the time + past simple, … past perfect`**: *By the time the police arrived, the thief **had escaped**.*
> - En reported speech, el past simple del estilo directo **sube** a past perfect.
> - `already, just, never` van **entre** *had* y el participio.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena con el past perfect
> 1. She couldn't pay because she ______ (forget) her wallet.
> 2. When I got home, my brother ______ already ______ (cook) dinner.
> 3. They told me they ______ (never/see) snow before.
> 4. By 2020 he ______ (write) three books.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **had forgotten** — 2. **had** already **cooked** — 3. **had never seen** — 4. **had written**

> [!question]+ 2. Elige: past simple o past perfect
> 1. After the film ______ (finish), we ______ (go) home.
> 2. The train ______ (leave) before we ______ (arrive).
> 3. I ______ (not/see) her since school when we met again.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **finished / went** (orden claro; ambos simple valen) — 2. **had left / arrived** — 3. **hadn't seen**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. When I had arrived, the party had started.
> 2. She said she has seen the film.
> 3. By the time we got there, they left.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. When I **arrived**, the party **had started** (solo la anterior en perfect) — 2. She said she **had seen** the film — 3. …they **had** left

> [!question]+ 4. Une con el tiempo correcto
> 1. I / not eat + so I / be / hungry
> 2. The match / already / start + when we / arrive

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. *I **hadn't eaten**, so I **was** hungry.* — 2. *The match **had** already **started** when we **arrived**.*
