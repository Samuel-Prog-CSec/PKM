<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El *past simple* narra acciones terminadas en un momento concreto del pasado</mark>. El momento puede estar dicho (*yesterday*) o sobreentendido.

# Estructura (verbos normales)

| Forma | Patrón | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | verbo en pasado (regular `-ed` / irregular) | She **worked** / She **went**. |
| Negativa | **didn't** + verbo base | She **didn't work**. |
| Interrogativa | **Did** + S + verbo base | **Did** she **work**? |

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Tras *did/didn't* el verbo vuelve a su forma base</mark>: *Did you went?* ❌ → *Did you go?* ✅.

# El verbo *to be* va por libre

`to be` no usa `did`: tiene sus propias formas **was / were**.

| | Afirmativa | Negativa | Pregunta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / he / she / it | **was** | wasn't | **Was** I…? |
| you / we / they | **were** | weren't | **Were** you…? |

*I **was** tired. They **weren't** at home. **Was** she happy?*

# Regulares: escritura del -ed

- La mayoría `+ -ed` → work → worked.
- `-e` → `+ -d` → like → liked.
- Consonante + `-y` → `-ied` → study → **studied** (pero play → played).
- CVC acentuada dobla → stop → **stopped**, plan → planned (pero *visit* → visited: no acentuada).

# Irregulares (hay que memorizarlos)

No siguen regla. Los más frecuentes:

| Base | Pasado | Base | Pasado |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| go | went | see | saw |
| have | had | do | did |
| make | made | take | took |
| get | got | come | came |
| buy | bought | think | thought |
| know | knew | give | gave |
| find | found | tell | told |

# Pronunciación de -ed (importa en listening y speaking)

- `/ɪd/` tras `t/d`: wanted, needed, decided.
- `/t/` tras sonido sordo (p, k, f, s, sh, ch): worked, watched, hoped.
- `/d/` en el resto (sonidos sonoros y vocales): played, lived, opened.

Palabras señal: `yesterday, last week/year, in 2015, two days ago, then, when I was young`.

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Doble pasado en negativa/pregunta: *I didn't saw* ❌ → *I didn't see* ✅.
> - Irregulares mal formados: *He buyed / goed* ❌ → *bought / went* ✅.
> - Mezclar *was/were*: *You was there* ❌ → *You **were** there* ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`ago`** va **detrás** del periodo: *two years ago* (no *ago two years*), y siempre con past simple, nunca present perfect.
> - **`when`** en preguntas exige past simple: *When did you arrive?* (no *When have you arrived?*).
> - **El pasado de `can` es `could`**; el de `must` es `had to` (ver [[01 - Habilidad - can, could, be able to]] y [[02 - Obligación y prohibición - must, have to]]).
> - Verbos que se escriben igual en presente y pasado (*cut, put, hit, read*): *read* cambia de pronunciación (/riːd/ → /rɛd/).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena (past simple; ojo con was/were)
> 1. We ______ (see) a great film last night.
> 2. She ______ (not/have) time to call.
> 3. They ______ (be) very tired after the trip.
> 4. I ______ (buy) this two days ago.
> 5. ______ he ______ (go) to the party?

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **saw** — 2. **didn't have** — 3. **were** — 4. **bought** — 5. **Did** he **go**

> [!question]+ 2. Elige la opción correcta
> 1. I ______ him yesterday. → (a) have seen (b) saw (c) seen
> 2. You ______ right. → (a) was (b) were (c) did
> 3. They ______ come to the party. → (a) didn't (b) don't (c) weren't

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **b** (yesterday → past simple) — 2. **b** — 3. **a**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. Did you went to school?
> 2. She buyed a new phone.
> 3. We was at home all day.
> 4. He didn't wanted to come.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. Did you **go**…? — 2. She **bought**… — 3. We **were**… — 4. He didn't **want**…

> [!question]+ 4. ¿Cuál es la frase CORRECTA?
> 1. (a) When have you arrived? (b) When did you arrive? (c) When you arrived?
> 2. (a) I studied ago three hours. (b) I studied three hours ago. (c) I have studied three hours ago.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **b** — 2. **b**

> [!question]+ 5. Escribe el pasado de estos irregulares
> think · give · find · take · know

> [!success]- Solución 5
> thought · gave · found · took · knew
