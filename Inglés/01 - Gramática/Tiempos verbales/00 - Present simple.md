<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El *present simple* expresa rutinas, hábitos, hechos permanentes y verdades generales</mark> — lo que ocurre siempre o de forma regular, no lo que pasa ahora mismo.

# Estructura

| Forma | Patrón | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativa | S + verbo (+ **-s** en *he/she/it*) | She **works** in Madrid. |
| Negativa | S + **don't / doesn't** + verbo base | They **don't work** here. |
| Interrogativa | **Do / Does** + S + verbo base | **Does** he **work**? |
| Respuesta corta | Yes, he **does**. / No, they **don't**. | — |

# La -s de tercera persona (reglas de escritura)

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">La `-s` de *he/she/it* es el error nº 1</mark>:

- La mayoría: `+ -s` → work → works.
- Verbos en `-o, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z`: `+ -es` → go → **goes**, watch → watches, kiss → kisses.
- Consonante + `-y` → `-ies` → study → **studies** (pero vocal + y: play → plays).
- Irregulares: have → **has**, do → **does** /dʌz/, go → **goes**.

# Cuándo se usa

- **Rutinas y hábitos:** *I get up at seven every day.*
- **Hechos permanentes / estados:** *She **lives** in Toledo. I **have** two brothers.*
- **Verdades generales:** *Water **boils** at 100 degrees.*
- **Horarios y programas (futuro fijo):** *The train **leaves** at 9:00.* (ver [[11 - El presente con valor de futuro]]).
- **Verbos de estado** (like, know, want, believe…): siempre en simple, aunque sea "ahora" (ver [[01 - Present continuous]]).

Palabras señal: `always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day, once a week, on Mondays`.

# Posición de los adverbios de frecuencia

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Van **antes** del verbo principal, pero **después** de *to be***: *I **always** get up early* / *She **is always** late* / *They **don't usually** eat out*. Con auxiliar, entre auxiliar y verbo.

# Casos atípicos

- **Instrucciones y recetas:** se usa present simple → *First you **mix** the eggs, then you **add** the flour.*
- **Comentar deportes/acciones en directo:** *Messi **passes** to Suárez and **scores**.*
- **Titulares de periódico:** *President **visits** Toledo* (aunque sea pasado).
- **Verbos de percepción con significado de estado** (see, hear): *I **see** what you mean* (no *am seeing*).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Olvidar la `-s`: *He work* ❌ → *He works* ✅.
> - Doblar la marca en preguntas/negativas: *Does he works?* ❌ → *Does he work?* ✅ (la `-s` ya la lleva *does*).
> - *don't* con tercera persona: *She don't like it* ❌ → *She doesn't like it* ✅.
> - Usar continuous para hábitos: *I am going to the gym every day* ❌ → *I go to the gym every day* ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`every day` (rutina) vs `everyday` (adjetivo = cotidiano):** *I read every day* ≠ *an everyday activity*.
> - **Sujetos que parecen plurales pero son singulares:** *Everybody, everyone, nobody, each, the news, mathematics* → verbo en `-s`: *Everybody **knows** it. The news **is** sad.*
> - **`How often…?`** pide frecuencia → responde en present simple.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena con el present simple
> 1. My sister ______ (study) medicine.
> 2. The shop ______ (not/open) on Sundays.
> 3. Water ______ (freeze) at zero degrees.
> 4. He ______ (go) to work by bike.
> 5. Everybody ______ (know) the answer.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **studies** — 2. **doesn't open** — 3. **freezes** — 4. **goes** — 5. **knows**

> [!question]+ 2. Elige la opción correcta (a/b/c)
> 1. She ______ tea. → (a) don't like (b) doesn't like (c) doesn't likes
> 2. ______ they live near you? → (a) Do (b) Does (c) Are
> 3. He always ______ late. → (a) arrives (b) arrive (c) is arrive
> 4. The train ______ at 8. → (a) leave (b) leaves (c) is leaving

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **b** — 2. **a** — 3. **a** — 4. **b**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. Does she works on Saturdays?
> 2. My brother don't eat fish.
> 3. She go to school by bus.
> 4. We always are busy on Mondays.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. Does she **work**…? — 2. …**doesn't** eat… — 3. She **goes**… — 4. We **are always** busy… (adverbio tras *to be*)

> [!question]+ 4. ¿Cuál es la frase CORRECTA?
> 1. (a) He watchs TV. (b) He watches TV. (c) He watch TV.
> 2. (a) Do he like it? (b) Does he likes it? (c) Does he like it?
> 3. (a) Everybody are here. (b) Everybody is here. (c) Everybody be here.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **b** — 2. **c** — 3. **b**

> [!question]+ 5. Ordena las palabras para formar una pregunta
> 1. often / how / you / do / exercise / ?
> 2. your / does / where / father / work / ?
> 3. eat / they / do / meat / ?

> [!success]- Solución 5
> 1. **How often do you exercise?** — 2. **Where does your father work?** — 3. **Do they eat meat?**
