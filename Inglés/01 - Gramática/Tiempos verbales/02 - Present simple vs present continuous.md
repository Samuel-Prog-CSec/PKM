La clave: <mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">el *simple* es para lo permanente o habitual; el *continuous* para lo temporal o en curso</mark>.

| Present simple | Present continuous |
| --- | --- |
| Hábito / rutina: *I **play** tennis on Sundays.* | Ahora mismo: *I**'m playing** tennis.* |
| Hecho permanente: *She **lives** in Toledo.* | Situación temporal: *She**'s living** with friends this month.* |
| Verdad general: *Water **freezes** at 0°C.* | Cambio/tendencia: *It**'s getting** cold.* |
| Horario fijo: *The film **starts** at 8.* | Plan acordado: *We**'re watching** a film tonight.* |
| Rutina neutra: *He **complains** a lot.* | Queja (always): *He**'s always complaining**.* |

# El desempate: stative verbs

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Muchos ejercicios se resuelven recordando que los verbos de estado van en simple</mark> aunque el contexto sea "ahora": *Right now I **want** to go home* (no *am wanting*). Y ojo con los que cambian de significado (*have, think, see, taste* — ver [[01 - Present continuous]]).

# Señales que te chivan el tiempo

- `always, usually, every day, on Mondays, how often` → **simple**.
- `now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!, right now` → **continuous**.
- `these days, this week, currently` → situación temporal → **continuous**.
- `today` → ambiguo: mira si es rutina (simple) o algo puntual de hoy (continuous).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - Frase con **verbo de estado + "now"**: la tentación es el continuous, pero va el **simple** (*Now I understand*).
> - **`always` + continuous** = queja, no rutina. Si el tono es de fastidio, continuous.
> - Un mismo verbo puede ir en los dos según signifique estado o acción (*think, have, see*).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena (simple o continuous)
> 1. My father ______ (work) in a bank, but this week he ______ (work) from home.
> 2. I ______ (think) you're right. (opinión)
> 3. Sh! I ______ (listen) to the news.
> 4. Dogs ______ (not/like) loud noises.
> 5. Prices ______ (go) up at the moment.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **works / is working** — 2. **think** (stative) — 3. **am listening** — 4. **don't like** — 5. **are going**

> [!question]+ 2. Elige la opción correcta
> 1. "What ______?" "I'm a teacher." → (a) do you do (b) are you doing
> 2. "What ______?" "I'm cooking." → (a) do you do (b) are you doing
> 3. He ______ his own house at the moment. → (a) builds (b) is building

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **a** (profesión = permanente) — 2. **b** (ahora) — 3. **b**

> [!question]+ 3. ¿Correcta (✔) o incorrecta (✘)? Corrige las falsas
> 1. She is having three sisters.
> 2. We are learning a lot this year.
> 3. I am agreeing with you.
> 4. Look — the bus is coming!

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. ✘ → She **has** three sisters — 2. ✔ — 3. ✘ → I **agree** with you — 4. ✔

> [!question]+ 4. Traduce
> 1. Normalmente cojo el metro, pero hoy voy andando.
> 2. ¿Qué estás leyendo?

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **I usually take the metro, but today I'm walking.** — 2. **What are you reading?**
