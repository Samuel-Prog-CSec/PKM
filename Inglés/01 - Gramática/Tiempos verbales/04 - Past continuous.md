<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El *past continuous* describe una acción que estaba en curso en un momento del pasado</mark>, a menudo interrumpida por otra o como telón de fondo de una historia.

# Estructura

**was / were + verbo-`ing`**. Negativa: wasn't/weren't + -ing. Interrogativa: Was/Were + S + -ing?
*I **was** studying. They **weren't** listening. **Was** she **working**?*

# Cuándo se usa

- **En curso en un momento pasado:** *At 8 p.m. I **was studying**.*
- **Acción interrumpida** (continuous = larga; simple = corta que interrumpe): *I **was cooking** when the phone **rang**.*
- **Dos acciones simultáneas:** *While she **was reading**, he **was watching** TV.*
- **Fondo de una narración:** *The sun **was shining** and birds **were singing** when it happened.*

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">`while` suele acompañar a la acción larga (continuous); `when`, a la corta (past simple)</mark>: *While I was walking… / …when it started to rain.*

# Diferencia clave con el past simple

- *When she arrived, we **had** dinner* (past simple + simple = **secuencia**: primero llegó, luego cenamos).
- *When she arrived, we **were having** dinner* (continuous = ya estábamos cenando cuando llegó).

# Casos atípicos

- **Peticiones y comentarios corteses:** *I **was wondering** if you could help me* (más suave que *I wonder*; ver [[05 - Permiso, peticiones y ofrecimientos]]).
- **No para hábitos pasados:** para "solía", va [[12 - used to]] o *would*, no el past continuous.

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Stative en continuo: *I was knowing* ❌ → *I knew* ✅.
> - Confundir *was/were*: *They was sleeping* ❌ → *They **were** sleeping* ✅.
> - Usar continuous para una acción única y puntual acabada: *Yesterday I was breaking my arm* ❌ → *I **broke** my arm* ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`while` casi siempre pide el continuous**; **`when`**, según el caso, simple o continuous.
> - Dos pasados con `when`: si la acción ya estaba pasando, continuous; si es secuencia, simple.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena con el past continuous
> 1. When I arrived, they ______ (have) dinner.
> 2. What ______ you ______ (do) at ten last night?
> 3. While he ______ (sleep), someone ______ (steal) his bike.
> 4. We ______ (not/pay) attention when the teacher explained it.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **were having** — 2. **were** you **doing** — 3. **was sleeping** / **stole** — 4. **weren't paying**

> [!question]+ 2. Elige: past simple o past continuous
> 1. She ______ (drive) home when she ______ (see) the accident.
> 2. The film ______ (start) at nine.
> 3. While we ______ (wait), it ______ (begin) to rain.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **was driving / saw** — 2. **started** — 3. **were waiting / began**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. I was knowing the answer.
> 2. They was watching TV.
> 3. When it rained, we were walking home and got wet. (¿tiene sentido el orden?)

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. I **knew** the answer (stative) — 2. They **were** watching TV — 3. Mejor: *We **were walking** home when it **started** to rain* (la acción larga en continuous)

> [!question]+ 4. Une las dos frases con when o while
> 1. I / cook + the lights / go out
> 2. She / study + her friends / play outside

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. *I **was cooking** when the lights **went** out.* — 2. *While she **was studying**, her friends **were playing** outside.*
