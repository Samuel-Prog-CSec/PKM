<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El *present perfect* conecta el pasado con el presente</mark>: la acción ocurrió antes, pero importa **ahora** (por su resultado, por ser experiencia, o porque continúa). Es el tiempo que más cuesta al hispanohablante porque no siempre equivale al pretérito perfecto español.

# Estructura

**have / has + participio pasado** (*past participle*, la 3ª columna). Regulares = `-ed`; irregulares hay que saberlos (go → gone, see → seen, do → done, be → been, write → written).
Negativa: haven't/hasn't + participio. Pregunta: Have/Has + S + participio?

# Los tres usos y sus marcadores

- **Experiencia** (en algún momento de la vida, sin decir cuándo): `ever`, `never`. *Have you **ever been** to London? I**'ve never tried** sushi.*
- **Pasado reciente con efecto presente:** `just`, `already`, `yet`. *I**'ve just finished**. She**'s already left**. Haven't you done it **yet**?*
- **Situación que empezó en el pasado y sigue:** `for` (duración) y `since` (punto de inicio). *We**'ve lived** here **for** five years / **since** 2021.*

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">`for` + periodo (for two hours), `since` + momento de inicio (since Monday, since I was ten)</mark>.

# Colocación de los adverbios

- `just, already` → **entre** *have* y el participio: *I**'ve just eaten*. She**'s already gone**.*
- `yet` → **al final** y solo en negativas/preguntas: *Have you finished **yet**? I haven't done it **yet**.*
- `ever/never` → entre *have* y el participio.

# been vs gone

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">*He's **been** to Paris* = fue y ya ha vuelto (experiencia). *He's **gone** to Paris* = se fue y **sigue allí**</mark>.

# Casos atípicos

- **`It's the first time…`** pide present perfect: *It's the first time I**'ve driven** abroad.*
- **Superlativo + present perfect:** *It's the best film I**'ve ever seen**.*
- **`This week / today / so far`** (periodos aún abiertos) admiten present perfect: *I**'ve had** three coffees today.*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Usar past simple con `since/for` de algo que sigue: *I live here for five years* ❌ → *I**'ve lived** here for five years* ✅.
> - Traducir el pretérito perfecto español a lo bruto: *I have seen him yesterday* ❌ → *I **saw** him yesterday* ✅ (ver [[06 - Present perfect vs past simple]]).
> - `yet` en afirmativa: *I have finished yet* ❌ → *I have **already** finished* ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`for` vs `since`:** *for* + duración, *since* + inicio. *since five years* ❌.
> - **`gone` vs `been`:** deciden si la persona sigue fuera o ya volvió.
> - **`How long…?`** sobre algo que continúa → present perfect, no present simple: *How long **have** you **lived** here?*

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena con el present perfect
> 1. I ______ (never/eat) octopus.
> 2. She ______ (live) in Toledo since March.
> 3. ______ you ______ (finish) your homework yet?
> 4. We ______ (just/arrive).
> 5. It's the best book I ______ (ever/read).

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **have never eaten** — 2. **has lived** — 3. **Have** you **finished** — 4. **have just arrived** — 5. **have ever read**

> [!question]+ 2. for o since
> 1. I've known her ______ 2010.
> 2. They've been married ______ ten years.
> 3. We haven't seen him ______ last summer.
> 4. She's been ill ______ three days.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **since** — 2. **for** — 3. **since** — 4. **for**

> [!question]+ 3. been o gone
> 1. Mum isn't here — she's ______ to the shops.
> 2. I've ______ to Italy twice; it's beautiful.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **gone** (sigue fuera) — 2. **been** (fui y volví)

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el error
> 1. I have finished my work yet.
> 2. She lives here since 2019.
> 3. Have you ever went to Japan?

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. …**already** finished (o *haven't finished yet*) — 2. She **has lived** here since 2019 — 3. Have you ever **been** to Japan?

> [!question]+ 5. ¿Cuál es la frase CORRECTA?
> 1. (a) I've seen her last week. (b) I saw her last week. (c) I've saw her last week.
> 2. (a) We're here since Monday. (b) We've been here since Monday. (c) We are here for Monday.

> [!success]- Solución 5
> 1. **b** — 2. **b**
