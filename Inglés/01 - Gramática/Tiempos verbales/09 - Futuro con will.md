<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">`will` es el futuro para predicciones, decisiones que tomas en el momento, promesas y ofrecimientos</mark>.

# Estructura

**will + verbo base** (invariable, todas las personas). Contracción `'ll`; negativa `won't`. *I**'ll** call you. She **won't** come. **Will** they help?*

# Cuándo se usa

- **Predicción / opinión sobre el futuro:** *I think it **will rain** tomorrow.*
- **Decisión espontánea** (justo al hablar): *The phone's ringing — I**'ll get** it.*
- **Promesa / ofrecimiento / negativa a hacer algo:** *I**'ll help** you. I **won't** tell anyone. He **won't** listen* (se niega).
- **Hechos futuros seguros:** *She **will be** 30 next month.*

Palabras señal: `probably, I think, I'm sure, I expect, maybe, perhaps, tomorrow, next…`.

# Con otras estructuras

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Tras `if` (primer condicional) y tras conjunciones temporales (`when, as soon as, until, before, after`) NO se usa `will`</mark>: va present simple, y `will` en la otra mitad: *If it **rains**, I**'ll** stay home. I**'ll** call you when I **arrive**.* (ver [[01 - First conditional]] y [[03 - Conjunciones temporales de futuro]]).

# Casos atípicos

- **`shall`** para ofrecimientos/sugerencias en 1ª persona: *__Shall__ I help? __Shall__ we go?* (ver [[05 - Permiso, peticiones y ofrecimientos]]).
- **`will` para peticiones:** *__Will__ you close the door?*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Añadir `-s` o `to`: *She wills / will to come* ❌ → *She **will** come* ✅.
> - Poner `will` tras `if`/`when`: *If it will rain* ❌ → *If it **rains*** ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **Decisión en el momento → `will`; plan previo → `going to`**: *"We've run out of milk." "OK, I**'ll** buy some."* (decides ahora) vs *I**'m going to** buy milk after work* (ya planeado). Ver [[10 - Futuro con going to]].
> - Con evidencia visible, se prefiere `going to`, no `will`.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena con will/won't
> 1. I'm sure you ______ (pass) the exam.
> 2. "It's cold." "I ______ (close) the window."
> 3. She ______ (not/be) at the meeting; she's ill.
> 4. If you ask him, he ______ (help) you.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **will pass** — 2. **'ll close** — 3. **won't be** — 4. **will help**

> [!question]+ 2. Elige: will o going to
> 1. Look at those clouds! It ______ rain. → (a) will (b) is going to
> 2. "The bag is heavy." "I ______ carry it for you." → (a) 'll (b) 'm going to
> 3. We ______ visit Rome next month; we've booked flights. → (a) will (b) are going to

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **b** (evidencia) — 2. **a** (decisión espontánea) — 3. **b** (plan)

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. If it will be sunny, we'll go out.
> 2. She will to help you.
> 3. I call you when I will arrive.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. If it **is** sunny… — 2. She will **help** you — 3. I'll call you when I **arrive**

> [!question]+ 4. ¿Cuál es la frase CORRECTA?
> 1. (a) I think it rains tomorrow. (b) I think it will rain tomorrow. (c) I think it going to rain tomorrow.
> 2. (a) Will you to marry me? (b) Do you will marry me? (c) Will you marry me?

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **b** — 2. **c**
