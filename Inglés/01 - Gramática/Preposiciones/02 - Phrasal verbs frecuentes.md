<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Un *phrasal verb* es un verbo + partícula (preposición o adverbio) cuyo significado no siempre se deduce de las palabras</mark>: *give up* = rendirse, *look after* = cuidar. Aparecen mucho en las partes 5 y 6 del reading.

# Separables vs inseparables

- **Separables:** el objeto puede ir en medio o al final: *turn **off** the TV / turn the TV **off**.* <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Si el objeto es un pronombre, va OBLIGATORIAMENTE en medio</mark>: *turn **it** off* ✅ / *turn off it* ❌.
- **Inseparables:** verbo y partícula no se separan: *look after the baby* (no *look the baby after*).

# Lista de phrasal verbs frecuentes (B1)

| Phrasal verb | Significado | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| give up | rendirse / dejar | *I **gave up** smoking.* |
| look after | cuidar | *She **looks after** her sister.* |
| look for | buscar | *I'm **looking for** my keys.* |
| look forward to | tener ganas de | *I **look forward to** seeing you.* |
| find out | averiguar | *We **found out** the truth.* |
| turn on/off | encender/apagar | *__Turn off__ the light.* |
| get on (with) | llevarse bien | *I **get on with** my boss.* |
| get up | levantarse | *I **get up** at 7.* |
| put on / take off | ponerse / quitarse | *__Put on__ your coat.* |
| pick up | recoger | *I'll **pick you up** at 8.* |
| run out of | quedarse sin | *We **ran out of** milk.* |
| go on | continuar / pasar | *__Go on__! What happened?* |
| deal with | ocuparse de | *I'll **deal with** it.* |

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **`look forward to`** lleva el *to* como preposición → **+ -ing**: *look forward to **seeing*** (no *to see*). Ver [[00 - Gerundios e infinitivos]].
- Un mismo phrasal puede tener **varios sentidos**: *take off* = despegar (avión) o quitarse (ropa).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Pronombre al final en separables: *turn off it* ❌ → *turn **it** off* ✅.
> - Separar los inseparables: *look the children after* ❌ → *look after the children* ✅.
> - *look forward to + infinitivo*: *to meet* ❌ → *to **meeting*** ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **Pronombre → siempre en medio** en los separables.
> - **look after ≠ look for ≠ look forward to** (cuidar / buscar / tener ganas).
> - La partícula cambia el sentido: *give up, give back, give in.*

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Completa con el phrasal verb correcto (give up, look after, find out, run out of, get on with)
> 1. Don't ______; keep trying!
> 2. Can you ______ the kids tonight?
> 3. We ______ petrol on the motorway.
> 4. I really ______ my classmates.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **give up** — 2. **look after** — 3. **ran out of** — 4. **get on with**

> [!question]+ 2. Coloca el pronombre
> 1. Turn off the radio. → Turn ______ ______.
> 2. Pick up the children. → Pick ______ ______.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. Turn **it off** — 2. Pick **them up**

> [!question]+ 3. look for, look after o look forward to
> 1. I'm ______ my phone; I can't find it.
> 2. She ______ her grandmother every weekend.
> 3. We ______ ______ the holidays.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **looking for** — 2. **looks after** — 3. **look forward to** (+ -ing)

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el error
> 1. Turn off it, please.
> 2. I look forward to meet you.
> 3. She looks the baby after.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. Turn **it off**, please — 2. …look forward to **meeting** you — 3. She **looks after** the baby
