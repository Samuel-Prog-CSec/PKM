# at / in / on para el lugar

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">`at` = punto, `in` = dentro de un espacio, `on` = sobre una superficie</mark>:

| Prep. | Idea | Ejemplos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **at** | punto/lugar concreto | at the bus stop, at home, at the door, at work |
| **in** | dentro de un espacio | in the room, in London, in a car, in bed |
| **on** | sobre una superficie/línea | on the table, on the wall, on the bus, on the second floor |

# Movimiento

- **to** = dirección (hacia): *go **to** school, walk **to** the park.*
- **into / out of** = entrar/salir: *come **into** the room, get **out of** the car.*
- **onto / off** = subir a / bajar de: *jump **onto** the stage, fall **off** the bike.*
- **through** = a través: *walk **through** the tunnel.* · **across** = de un lado a otro: *swim **across** the river.* · **along** = a lo largo: *walk **along** the street.*

# Transporte: in vs on (para pillar)

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">`by` para el medio (by car, by bus), pero `in a car` y `on a bus/train/plane`</mark>: coches pequeños → *in*; transporte público grande → *on*. *get **in/out of** a car* pero *get **on/off** a bus*.

# Casos atípicos

- **at home / at school / at work** (sin artículo, punto de actividad) vs **in the house** (el edificio).
- **arrive**: *arrive **at*** (un punto: at the station) / *arrive **in*** (ciudad/país: in Spain) — nunca *arrive to*.
- **on the left/right, on the corner, on the coast.**

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - *arrive to*: *We arrived to Madrid* ❌ → *arrived **in** Madrid* ✅.
> - `in` con transporte público: *in the bus* ❌ → *__on__ the bus* ✅.
> - `to` con *home*: *go to home* ❌ → *go **home*** ✅ (sin preposición).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **arrive at (punto) / arrive in (ciudad-país)**, nunca *arrive to*.
> - **on a bus/train/plane; in a car/taxi.**
> - **go home** (sin *to*).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. at, in u on (lugar)
> 1. She's waiting ______ the bus stop.
> 2. The keys are ______ the table.
> 3. I live ______ the third floor.
> 4. He's ______ home right now.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **at** — 2. **on** — 3. **on** — 4. **at**

> [!question]+ 2. Preposición de movimiento (to, into, through, across, onto)
> 1. We walked ______ the forest.
> 2. She jumped ______ the pool.
> 3. Let's swim ______ the lake to the other side.
> 4. I'm going ______ the shops.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **through** — 2. **into** — 3. **across** — 4. **to**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. We arrived to the airport late.
> 2. I read a book in the train.
> 3. She goes to home at five.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. …arrived **at** the airport — 2. …**on** the train — 3. She goes **home** at five

> [!question]+ 4. by, in u on (transporte)
> 1. I go to work ______ bus.
> 2. They're already ______ the plane.
> 3. She arrived ______ a taxi.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **by** — 2. **on** — 3. **in**
