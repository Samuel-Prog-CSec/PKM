<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Una oración de relativo añade información sobre un sustantivo usando un pronombre relativo</mark> (who, which, that, whose, where, when).

# Los pronombres relativos

| Pronombre | Para | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **who** | personas | The man **who** called… |
| **which** | cosas/animales | The book **which** I read… |
| **that** | personas o cosas | The car **that** broke down… |
| **whose** | posesión | The girl **whose** dog… |
| **where** | lugares | The city **where** I live… |
| **when** | tiempo | The day **when** we met… |

# Especificativas vs explicativas

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">La coma lo cambia todo</mark>:

- **Especificativa** (sin comas): la información es **necesaria** para identificar. *The man **who** lives next door is a doctor* (¿qué hombre? el de al lado).
- **Explicativa** (entre comas): información **extra** que se puede quitar. *My father, **who** is 60, still works.*

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">En las explicativas NO se puede usar `that`</mark> ni omitir el pronombre: *My car, that is red…* ❌ → *…, **which** is red, …* ✅.

# Cuándo se omite el pronombre

Se puede **omitir** el relativo cuando es el **objeto** de la oración especificativa: *The film (that) I saw was great.* (I es el sujeto; *that* es objeto → se quita). No se omite si es **sujeto**: *The man **who** called* (no se quita).

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **`whose`** = "cuyo", también para cosas: *a house **whose** roof is red.*
- Preposición al final: *the person (**who/that**) I spoke **to*** (informal) / *the person **to whom** I spoke* (formal).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `that` en explicativas: *Ana, that is my sister…* ❌ → *Ana, **who** is my sister…* ✅.
> - Doble sujeto: *The man who he called me* ❌ → *The man **who** called me* ✅ (no repitas el pronombre).
> - `who` para cosas / `which` para personas.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **Con comas → which/who** (nunca *that*, ni omisión).
> - **Se omite el relativo solo si es objeto** de una especificativa.
> - No repetir el sujeto: *the book which I read **it*** ❌.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Completa con who, which, whose o where
> 1. That's the woman ______ won the prize.
> 2. This is the house ______ I was born.
> 3. He's the boy ______ father is a pilot.
> 4. The phone ______ I bought is broken.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **who** — 2. **where** — 3. **whose** — 4. **which/that**

> [!question]+ 2. ¿Se puede omitir el relativo? (Sí/No)
> 1. The book that I read…
> 2. The man who called…
> 3. The film which won the Oscar…

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **Sí** (objeto) — 2. **No** (sujeto) — 3. **No** (sujeto)

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. My brother, that lives in Rome, is a chef.
> 2. The car who I bought is fast.
> 3. The woman who she helped me was kind.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. …, **who** lives in Rome, … — 2. The car **which/that** I bought… — 3. The woman who **helped** me… (quita *she*)

> [!question]+ 4. Une las dos frases con un relativo
> 1. This is the restaurant. We had dinner there.
> 2. She's the teacher. Her class is the best.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. *This is the restaurant **where** we had dinner.* — 2. *She's the teacher **whose** class is the best.*
