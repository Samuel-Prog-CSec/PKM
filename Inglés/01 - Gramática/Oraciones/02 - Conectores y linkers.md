<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Los conectores enlazan ideas y son lo que sube la nota del writing</mark>: hacen que el texto fluya en vez de ser frases sueltas.

# Los grupos básicos

| Función | Conectores | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Añadir | and, also, too, as well, in addition, moreover | *It's cheap **and** fast.* |
| Contraste | but, however, although, though, even though, despite, in spite of | *It's small **but** comfortable.* |
| Causa | because, as, since, because of | *I stayed home **because** I was ill.* |
| Consecuencia | so, therefore, as a result | *It rained, **so** we stayed in.* |
| Propósito | to, in order to, so that | *I called **to** apologise.* |
| Secuencia | first, then, after that, finally | *First…, **then**…, **finally**…* |

# because vs so (causa vs consecuencia)

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">`because` introduce la **causa**; `so`, la **consecuencia**</mark>: *I was tired, **so** I went to bed* = *I went to bed **because** I was tired.* (misma idea, orden inverso).

# although / though / despite / in spite of (contraste)

- **`although / though / even though`** + **frase** (sujeto + verbo): *__Although__ it was cold, we went out.*
- **`despite / in spite of`** + **sustantivo o `-ing`**: *__Despite__ the cold / __despite__ being cold, we went out.*

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">El error clásico: `despite of`</mark> ❌ → *despite* (sin *of*) o *in spite **of***.

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **`however`** va con punto o punto y coma, no como *but*: *It was expensive. **However**, I bought it.*
- **`so that`** + frase con modal (propósito): *I left early **so that** I **could** catch the train.*
- **`because` ≠ `because of`**: *because* + frase; *because of* + sustantivo (*because of the rain*).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - *despite of*: elimina el *of* (o usa *in spite of*).
> - `although` + sustantivo: *Although the rain* ❌ → *Although **it rained*** / *Despite the rain* ✅.
> - Empezar frase con *But/And* en writing formal (mejor *However/In addition*).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **although/though + frase**; **despite/in spite of + nombre o -ing**.
> - **because + frase**; **because of + nombre**.
> - **so = consecuencia; because = causa.**

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Elige el conector
> 1. ______ it was raining, we went for a walk. → (a) Although (b) Despite
> 2. We stayed in ______ the storm. → (a) because (b) because of
> 3. I was tired, ______ I went to bed. → (a) because (b) so

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **a** (+ frase) — 2. **b** (+ nombre) — 3. **b**

> [!question]+ 2. although, despite o however
> 1. ______ being tired, she finished the race.
> 2. ______ she was tired, she finished the race.
> 3. It was hard. ______, she succeeded.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **Despite** — 2. **Although** — 3. **However**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. Despite of the rain, we went out.
> 2. Although the cold weather, we swam.
> 3. I stayed home because of I was ill.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **Despite** the rain (o *In spite of*) — 2. **Despite/In spite of** the cold weather (o *Although it was cold*) — 3. …**because** I was ill

> [!question]+ 4. Une las frases con el conector indicado
> 1. It was expensive. I bought it. (although)
> 2. I studied hard. I passed. (so)

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. *__Although__ it was expensive, I bought it.* — 2. *I studied hard, **so** I passed.*
