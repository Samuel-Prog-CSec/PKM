# Formar preguntas

El orden estándar es **(Wh-) + auxiliar + sujeto + verbo**:

- **Sí/No:** *__Do__ you like tea? __Is__ she coming? __Have__ you finished?*
- **Con wh- (what, where, when, why, how):** *__Where__ do you live? __What__ is he doing?*

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Si no hay otro auxiliar, se usa `do/does/did`</mark>: *Do you know? Did they come?* (con *be* y modales no hace falta: *Are you…? Can you…?*).

# Preguntas de sujeto (¡sin do!)

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Cuando el pronombre interrogativo ES el sujeto (who/what), NO se usa `do` y el verbo va en afirmativa</mark>: *__Who__ **broke** the window?* (no *Who did break*). *__What__ **happened**?*

Compara: *Who **called** you?* (sujeto, sin do) vs *Who **did** you **call**?* (objeto, con do).

# Question tags (¿verdad?)

Son mini-preguntas al final para confirmar: *You're Spanish, **aren't you**? He didn't call, **did he**?*

Regla: <mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">afirmativa → tag negativa; negativa → tag afirmativa</mark>. El tag repite el **auxiliar** (o *do/does/did*) y el pronombre.

| Frase | Tag |
| --- | --- |
| You **are** tired, | **aren't you**? |
| She **can** swim, | **can't she**? |
| They **don't** know, | **do they**? |
| He **went**, | **didn't he**? |

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **`I am` → tag `aren't I`** (irregular): *I'm late, **aren't I**?*
- **Imperativo → `will you`**: *Close the door, **will you**?* · **Let's → `shall we`**: *Let's go, **shall we**?*
- Sujetos *everybody/someone* → tag con **they**: *Somebody called, **didn't they**?*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `do` en preguntas de sujeto: *Who did break it?* ❌ → *Who **broke** it?* ✅.
> - Tag con la misma polaridad: *You're ok, are you?* ❌ (salvo tono especial) → *…, **aren't** you?* ✅.
> - Olvidar el auxiliar correcto en el tag.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **Pregunta de sujeto (who/what = sujeto) → sin `do`.**
> - **Tag = polaridad invertida** + mismo auxiliar + pronombre.
> - **`I'm …, aren't I?`** y **imperativo + `will you?`**.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Ordena para formar la pregunta
> 1. you / where / do / live / ?
> 2. she / is / what / doing / ?
> 3. broke / who / the / window / ?

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **Where do you live?** — 2. **What is she doing?** — 3. **Who broke the window?**

> [!question]+ 2. Añade la question tag
> 1. You're a student, ______?
> 2. She can't drive, ______?
> 3. They went home, ______?
> 4. I'm right, ______?

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **aren't you** — 2. **can she** — 3. **didn't they** — 4. **aren't I**

> [!question]+ 3. ¿de sujeto o de objeto? Elige
> 1. Who ______ this cake? → (a) made (b) did make
> 2. Who ______ you invite? → (a) invited (b) did you invite

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **a** (sujeto) — 2. **b** (objeto)

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el error
> 1. Who did win the match?
> 2. You like coffee, don't you like?
> 3. Where you are going?

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. Who **won** the match? — 2. …, **don't you**? — 3. Where **are you** going?
