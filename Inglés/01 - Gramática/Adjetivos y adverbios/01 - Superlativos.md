<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El superlativo señala el extremo dentro de un grupo: el más / el menos de todos</mark>.

# Formación

Misma lógica de longitud que el comparativo, con **`the`** delante:

| Adjetivo | Superlativo |
| --- | --- |
| 1 sílaba | the **-est** (the tallest) |
| 2 síl. en `-y` | the **-iest** (the happiest) |
| 2+ sílabas | the **most** (the most modern) |
| inferioridad | the **least** (the least expensive) |

*It's **the tallest** building. She's **the most intelligent** student.*

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">El superlativo siempre lleva `the`</mark> (u otro determinante posesivo): *my **best** friend, the **worst** day.*

# Irregulares

good → **the best** · bad → **the worst** · far → **the farthest/furthest** · little → the least · much/many → the most.

# Estructuras frecuentes

- **`one of the + superlativo + sustantivo en plural`**: *It's **one of the best films** I've ever seen.* (¡plural!).
- **Superlativo + present perfect + ever**: *the **best** book I**'ve ever read**.*
- **`in`** para lugares/grupos (no *of*): *the tallest **in** the class, the biggest city **in** Spain.*

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- Tras *one of the*, el sustantivo va en **plural** aunque hablemos de uno: *one of the **tallest** buildings*.
- Para grupos usamos **`in`**, no *of*: *the best player **in** the team*.

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Olvidar `the`: *She's best student* ❌ → *…**the** best…* ✅.
> - Doble superlativo: *the most tallest* ❌.
> - `of` por `in`: *the biggest of the world* ❌ → *…**in** the world* ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`one of the` + superlativo + PLURAL**.
> - **`the` obligatorio** con superlativos.
> - Grupos/lugares → **`in`**, no *of*.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Escribe el superlativo
> 1. Everest is ______ (high) mountain in the world.
> 2. It was ______ (bad) day of my life.
> 3. She's ______ (intelligent) person I know.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **the highest** — 2. **the worst** — 3. **the most intelligent**

> [!question]+ 2. Completa la estructura one of the...
> 1. It's one of ______ (good / film) I've seen.
> 2. He's one of ______ (rich / man) in the country.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **the best films** — 2. **the richest men**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. She's the most tall in the class.
> 2. It's the better restaurant in town.
> 3. Paris is one of the most beautiful city in Europe.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. …**the tallest**… — 2. …the **best** restaurant… — 3. …one of the most beautiful **cities**…

> [!question]+ 4. ¿Cuál es la frase CORRECTA?
> 1. (a) the biggest of the world (b) the biggest in the world
> 2. (a) my more good friend (b) my best friend

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **b** — 2. **b**
