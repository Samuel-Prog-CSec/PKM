<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El **adjetivo** describe un sustantivo (a **quick** car); el **adverbio** describe un verbo, un adjetivo u otro adverbio (he drives **quickly**)</mark>.

# Formación del adverbio

Regla general: **adjetivo + `-ly`** → quick → quickly, careful → carefully.

Escritura: `-y → -ily` (happy → happily); `-le → -ly` (terrible → terribly); `-ic → -ically` (basic → basically). Excepción: *public → **publicly*** (no *publically*).

# Irregulares y trampas

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Algunos adverbios no acaban en `-ly` o cambian de forma</mark>:

| Adjetivo | Adverbio |
| --- | --- |
| good | **well** |
| fast | **fast** (igual) |
| hard | **hard** (igual) |
| late | **late** |
| early | **early** |

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">`hardly` y `lately` existen pero significan otra cosa</mark>: *hardly* = apenas (*I **hardly** know him*); *lately* = últimamente. No son el adverbio de *hard/late*.

# Adjetivo tras verbos de estado (linking verbs)

Tras `be, seem, look, feel, taste, smell, sound, become` va **adjetivo**, no adverbio, porque describen el sujeto: *You look **tired** (no tiredly). It smells **good** (no well).*

# Posición de los adverbios de frecuencia

`always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never`: **antes** del verbo principal, **después** de *to be*, **entre** auxiliar y verbo: *I **always** try. She **is** **never** late. I **have never** been there.*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Adverbio tras linking verb: *You look tiredly* ❌ → *You look **tired*** ✅.
> - `good` como adverbio: *She sings good* ❌ → *She sings **well*** ✅.
> - Confundir *hard* (duro) con *hardly* (apenas).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`good` (adj) / `well` (adv)**: *a good singer* / *sings well*.
> - **Tras look/feel/seem/taste → adjetivo**.
> - **`hardly` = apenas** (no "duramente"); es casi negativo.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. ¿Adjetivo o adverbio?
> 1. She speaks English ______ (perfect).
> 2. He's a ______ (careful) driver.
> 3. They arrived ______ (late).
> 4. The soup tastes ______ (delicious).

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **perfectly** — 2. **careful** — 3. **late** — 4. **delicious** (tras *taste*)

> [!question]+ 2. good o well
> 1. He plays the guitar very ______.
> 2. That's a ______ idea.
> 3. She didn't feel ______ yesterday.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **well** — 2. **good** — 3. **well** (feel well = de salud)

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. You sing beautiful.
> 2. He drives very careful.
> 3. She looks angrily today.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. You sing **beautifully** — 2. …very **carefully** — 3. She looks **angry** today

> [!question]+ 4. hard o hardly
> 1. He works very ______ (mucho).
> 2. I ______ ever watch TV (casi nunca).

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **hard** — 2. **hardly**
