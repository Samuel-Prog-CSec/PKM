# too y enough (exceso y suficiencia)

- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">`too` + adjetivo/adverbio = **demasiado** (un problema)</mark>: *It's **too** expensive. You drive **too** fast.*
- <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">`enough` = **suficiente**; va **después** del adjetivo pero **antes** del sustantivo</mark>: *old **enough**, **enough** money.*
- Con infinitivo: *too + adj + to* / *adj + enough + to*: *It's **too hot to** drink. He's **tall enough to** reach it.*

`too much / too many` = demasiado (con sustantivos, ver [[03 - Cuantificadores de cantidad]]).

# so y such (tan / tanto)

Ambos intensifican, pero la estructura cambia:

| Estructura | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- |
| **so + adjetivo/adverbio** | *It's **so** cold! She runs **so** fast.* |
| **such + (a/an) + (adj) + sustantivo** | *It's **such a** cold day! They're **such** nice people.* |
| **so much/many + sustantivo** | *There's **so much** noise.* |

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">La clave: `so` va con adjetivo solo; `such` va con un sustantivo (con su artículo)</mark>: *so nice* vs *such a nice person*.

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **`so … that` / `such … that`** (consecuencia): *It was **so** cold **that** we stayed in. It was **such a** good film **that** I watched it twice.*
- **`too` ≠ `very`**: *too* es negativo (exceso, impide algo); *very* solo intensifica. *It's very hot* (mucho calor) vs *It's too hot to go out* (tanto que no salgo).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `so` con sustantivo: *so a nice day* ❌ → *such a nice day* ✅.
> - `such` con adjetivo solo: *such cold* ❌ → *so cold* ✅.
> - `enough` antes del adjetivo: *enough tall* ❌ → *tall enough* ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`so` + adjetivo / `such (a)` + (adj +) sustantivo.**
> - **`enough`**: tras adjetivo, antes de sustantivo.
> - **`too` = negativo** (exceso); **`very` = intensidad** neutra.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. so o such
> 1. It was ______ a boring film.
> 2. The exam was ______ difficult.
> 3. They have ______ nice children.
> 4. She was driving ______ fast.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **such** — 2. **so** — 3. **such** — 4. **so**

> [!question]+ 2. too o enough (coloca bien)
> 1. This coffee is ______ hot ______ drink.
> 2. He isn't ______ (old) to vote.
> 3. We don't have ______ (time).

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **too** hot **to** drink — 2. old **enough** — 3. **enough** time

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. It's such cold today.
> 2. She's not enough tall for the team.
> 3. It was so a good idea.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. It's **so** cold today — 2. …not **tall enough**… — 3. It was **such** a good idea

> [!question]+ 4. Une con so...that o such...that
> 1. The bag was heavy. I couldn't lift it.
> 2. It was a good book. I finished it in a day.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. *The bag was **so heavy that** I couldn't lift it.* — 2. *It was **such a good book that** I finished it in a day.*
