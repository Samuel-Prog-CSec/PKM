Muchos verbos rigen una forma fija cuando les sigue otro verbo. Hay que memorizar los grupos.

# Verbos + GERUNDIO (-ing)

`enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, practise, imagine, keep, miss, admit, deny, recommend, feel like, can't help, can't stand, give up, look forward to`.

*I **enjoy reading**. Do you **mind waiting**? I **can't stand queuing**.*

# Verbos + INFINITIVO (to)

`want, need, decide, hope, expect, promise, offer, agree, refuse, learn, plan, would like, manage, afford, seem, pretend`.

*I **want to go**. She **decided to leave**. We **can't afford to buy** it.*

# Verbo + objeto + infinitivo

`tell, ask, want, advise, allow, teach, invite, remind, encourage` + persona + to: *He **told me to wait**. They **invited us to come**.*

# Admiten los dos SIN cambio (like, love, hate, start, begin, continue)

*I **like cooking** / I **like to cook*** (casi lo mismo). Con `would`, siempre infinitivo: *I**'d like to** cook.*

# Cambian de SIGNIFICADO (¡para pillar!)

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Con estos verbos, gerundio e infinitivo significan cosas distintas</mark>:

| Verbo | + gerundio | + infinitivo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **stop** | dejar de hacer: *stop **smoking*** | parar para hacer: *stop **to smoke*** |
| **remember** | recordar algo hecho: *remember **locking*** | acordarse de hacer: *remember **to lock*** |
| **forget** | olvidar algo hecho | olvidarse de hacer |
| **try** | probar (experimento): *try **restarting*** | intentar (esfuerzo): *try **to open*** |

*I **stopped smoking*** (lo dejé) vs *I **stopped to smoke*** (paré para fumar).

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - `would like` + gerundio: *I'd like going* ❌ → *I'd like **to go*** ✅.
> - Mezclar los grupos: *I enjoy to read* ❌ → *I enjoy **reading*** ✅; *I want going* ❌ → *I want **to go*** ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **enjoy/avoid/finish/mind/suggest → -ing**; **want/decide/hope/agree → to**.
> - **stop/remember/forget/try** cambian de sentido según la forma.
> - **would like → to** (aunque *like* solo admita las dos).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Gerundio o infinitivo
> 1. I enjoy ______ (cook).
> 2. She decided ______ (study) abroad.
> 3. Would you mind ______ (open) the window?
> 4. We hope ______ (see) you soon.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **cooking** — 2. **to study** — 3. **opening** — 4. **to see**

> [!question]+ 2. stop/remember/try — elige según el sentido
> 1. Remember ______ (buy) milk on the way home. (no lo olvides)
> 2. I stopped ______ (work) because I was tired. (lo dejé)
> 3. Try ______ (restart) the router; maybe it helps. (experimenta)

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **to buy** — 2. **working** — 3. **restarting**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. I'd like reading more.
> 2. He avoided to answer the question.
> 3. They invited us come to dinner.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. I'd like **to read** more — 2. …avoided **answering**… — 3. …invited us **to come**…

> [!question]+ 4. ¿Cuál es la frase CORRECTA?
> 1. (a) I can't stand to wait. (b) I can't stand waiting.
> 2. (a) He promised helping. (b) He promised to help.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **b** — 2. **b**
