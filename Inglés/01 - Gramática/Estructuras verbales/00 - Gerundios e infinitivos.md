<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Cuando un verbo va detrás de otra palabra, aparece en **gerundio** (`-ing`) o en **infinitivo** (`to + base`)</mark>. Elegir bien depende de qué lo precede.

# Se usa GERUNDIO (-ing)

- **Como sujeto** de la frase: ***Smoking** is bad for you. **Swimming** keeps you fit.*
- **Tras preposición** (in, on, at, of, about, for, without, before, after…): *I'm good **at cooking**. She left **without saying** goodbye. Thanks **for helping**.*
- **Tras ciertos verbos** (enjoy, avoid, finish, mind…): ver [[01 - Verbos seguidos de gerundio o infinitivo]].

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Tras preposición SIEMPRE va gerundio, nunca infinitivo</mark>: *interested in **to learn*** ❌ → *interested in **learning*** ✅.

# Se usa INFINITIVO (to)

- **Para expresar finalidad** ("para"): *I went out **to buy** milk.* (no *for buy*).
- **Tras muchos adjetivos**: *It's easy **to learn**. I'm happy **to help**. Nice **to meet** you.*
- **Tras ciertos verbos** (want, decide, hope, need…): ver la nota siguiente.
- **Tras `too` y `enough`**: *too tired **to walk**, old enough **to drive**.*

# Infinitivo sin to

Va **sin `to`** tras modales (*can go*), tras `let/make` (*She **made** me **wait***) y tras `would rather / had better`.

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **Finalidad = `to` (o `in order to`), no `for` + infinitivo**: *I came here **to study*** (no *for study*). *For* + `-ing` explica la función de una cosa: *This tool is **for opening** bottles.*
- **`to` como preposición** en *look forward to, be used to*: lleva **-ing**: *I look forward **to seeing** you.*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Infinitivo tras preposición: *good at to cook* ❌ → *good at **cooking*** ✅.
> - `for` + infinitivo para finalidad: *I stopped for buy bread* ❌ → *…**to buy** bread* ✅.
> - Gerundio como sujeto olvidado: *Swim is fun* ❌ → ***Swimming** is fun* ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **Tras preposición → -ing** (sin excepción).
> - **Finalidad → to + base** (no *for*).
> - **`look forward to / be used to` + -ing** (ese *to* es preposición).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Gerundio o infinitivo
> 1. ______ (learn) languages is useful.
> 2. She's afraid of ______ (fly).
> 3. I went to the shop ______ (buy) milk.
> 4. He's old enough ______ (drive).

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **Learning** — 2. **flying** — 3. **to buy** — 4. **to drive**

> [!question]+ 2. Elige la opción correcta
> 1. Thanks for ______ me. → (a) to help (b) helping
> 2. I look forward to ______ you. → (a) see (b) seeing
> 3. It's important ______ on time. → (a) to be (b) being

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **b** — 2. **b** — 3. **a**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. I'm interested in to learn Chinese.
> 2. She stopped for talk to me.
> 3. Swim every day is healthy.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. …interested in **learning**… — 2. …stopped **to talk**… — 3. **Swimming** every day is healthy

> [!question]+ 4. Traduce
> 1. Salí para comprar pan.
> 2. Estoy cansado de esperar.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **I went out to buy bread.** — 2. **I'm tired of waiting.**
