<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">La voz pasiva pone el foco en **quién recibe la acción**, no en quién la hace</mark>. El objeto de la activa pasa a ser sujeto.

*Active:* *Shakespeare **wrote** Hamlet.* → *Passive:* *Hamlet **was written** by Shakespeare.*

# Estructura: be + participio pasado

El verbo `be` va en el **tiempo original**; el verbo principal en **participio**:

| Tiempo | Pasiva | Ejemplo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present simple | am/is/are + part. | English **is spoken** here. |
| Past simple | was/were + part. | The house **was built** in 1990. |
| Present continuous | am/is/are being + part. | My car **is being repaired**. |
| Present perfect | have/has been + part. | The work **has been done**. |
| Futuro (will) | will be + part. | It **will be finished** tomorrow. |
| Modal | modal + be + part. | It **can be seen** from here. |

# Cuándo se usa

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Se prefiere la pasiva cuando el agente es desconocido, obvio o irrelevante</mark>: *My bike **was stolen*** (no sé quién). *He **was arrested*** (por la policía, es obvio).

# El agente con by

Solo se menciona si aporta información: *The novel **was written by** a teenager.* Si es irrelevante, se omite: *The road **is being repaired*** (¿por quién? da igual).

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- **Verbos con dos objetos** (give, send, tell): el sujeto pasivo suele ser la persona: *I **was given** a present* (más que *A present was given to me*).
- Los **verbos intransitivos** (sin objeto: *arrive, happen, die*) **no tienen pasiva**: *An accident was happened* ❌.

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Olvidar el participio (usar base o pasado): *It was write* ❌ → *It was **written*** ✅.
> - Concordar mal *be*: *The books was sold* ❌ → *The books **were** sold* ✅.
> - Pasiva de verbos intransitivos: *He was died* ❌ → *He **died*** ✅.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`be` lleva el tiempo; el verbo principal va en participio.**
> - **`by`** solo si el agente importa.
> - Verbos intransitivos (happen, arrive, die) **no** se pasivizan.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Pon en pasiva
> 1. Someone cleans the office every day. → The office ______ every day.
> 2. They built this bridge in 1920. → This bridge ______ in 1920.
> 3. They have sold the house. → The house ______.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **is cleaned** — 2. **was built** — 3. **has been sold**

> [!question]+ 2. Rellena con la pasiva correcta
> 1. English ______ (speak) in many countries.
> 2. The results ______ (announce) next week (futuro).
> 3. My car ______ (repair) at the moment.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **is spoken** — 2. **will be announced** — 3. **is being repaired**

> [!question]+ 3. Corrige el error
> 1. The letter was wrote yesterday.
> 2. The windows was broken.
> 3. The accident was happened at night.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. …was **written**… — 2. The windows **were** broken — 3. The accident **happened**…

> [!question]+ 4. ¿activa o pasiva? Elige la más natural
> 1. (a) Someone stole my phone. (b) My phone was stolen.
> 2. Shakespeare (a) wrote / (b) was written many plays.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **b** (agente desconocido) — 2. **a** (activa: Shakespeare es el agente)
