<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El *reported speech* cuenta lo que alguien dijo sin repetir sus palabras exactas</mark>. Al pasar de estilo directo a indirecto, los tiempos "retroceden".

# El salto de tiempos (backshift)

Cuando el verbo introductorio va en pasado (*said, told*), el verbo citado retrocede un paso:

| Directo | Indirecto |
| --- | --- |
| present simple | past simple |
| present continuous | past continuous |
| past simple | past perfect |
| present perfect | past perfect |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| must | had to |

*"I **am** tired" → She said she **was** tired.* · *"I **saw** him" → He said he **had seen** him.*

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Los modales que ya son "pasados" (`would, could, should, might, ought to`) no cambian</mark>: *"I would help" → He said he **would** help.* Repasa [[08 - Past perfect]], [[03 - Past simple]] y [[04 - Past continuous]].

# Pronombres y expresiones de tiempo/lugar

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Cambian los pronombres y los marcadores de tiempo/lugar</mark>: *now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there, this → that*.

*"I'll call you **tomorrow**" → She said she'd call me **the next day**.*

# say vs tell

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">`tell` necesita destinatario; `say` no</mark>: *She **told me** that… / She **said** that…* — *She said me* ❌, *She told that* ❌ (sin persona).

# Preguntas y órdenes en indirecto

- **Preguntas:** se pierde la inversión y (en preguntas sí/no) se usa `if/whether`: *"Where do you live?" → He asked me **where I lived**. "Are you ok?" → She asked **if I was ok**.*
- **Órdenes/peticiones:** `tell/ask + persona + to + infinitivo`: *"Sit down" → He told me **to sit down**. "Don't move" → She told me **not to move**.*

# Casos atípicos (para pillar)

- Si el verbo introductorio va en **presente** (*says*) o la información **sigue siendo verdad**, el backshift es opcional: *She says she **is** tired.*
- Las **verdades generales** no retroceden: *He said water **boils** at 100°C.*

> [!warning]+ Fallos típicos
> - Mantener la inversión en preguntas indirectas: *He asked where **did I live*** ❌ → *…where **I lived*** ✅.
> - *say me* / *tell without person*: usa *tell me* o *say (that)*.
> - Olvidar el backshift con verbo introductorio en pasado.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **Pregunta indirecta = orden de afirmativa** (sin *do/did*, sin inversión).
> - **say (sin persona) / tell + persona.**
> - Órdenes → **to + infinitivo**; negativas → **not to**.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Pasa a estilo indirecto (introductorio en pasado)
> 1. "I'm hungry." → She said she ______.
> 2. "I saw the film." → He said he ______ the film.
> 3. "I'll help you." → She said she ______ me.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **was hungry** — 2. **had seen** — 3. **would help**

> [!question]+ 2. say o tell
> 1. She ______ me she was busy.
> 2. He ______ that he was sorry.
> 3. They ______ us to wait.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **told** — 2. **said** — 3. **told**

> [!question]+ 3. Preguntas y órdenes indirectas
> 1. "Where are you from?" → She asked me where ______.
> 2. "Do you like it?" → He asked ______ I liked it.
> 3. "Close the door." → She told me ______ the door.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **I was from** — 2. **if/whether** — 3. **to close**

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el error
> 1. He asked me where did I live.
> 2. She said me she was tired.
> 3. He told to wait.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. …where **I lived** — 2. She **told** me / **said** she was tired — 3. He told **me** to wait
