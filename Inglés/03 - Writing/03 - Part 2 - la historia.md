<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">La historia te da la **primera frase** y tienes que continuarla</mark>. Es la otra opción de la Parte 2; premia la narración con tiempos del pasado y conectores de secuencia.

# La clave: los tiempos del pasado

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Una buena historia combina past simple (la acción), past continuous (el fondo) y past perfect (lo anterior)</mark>:

*It **was raining** *(fondo)* when I **left** *(acción)* home. I **had forgotten** *(anterior)* my umbrella.*

Repasa [[03 - Past simple]], [[04 - Past continuous]] y [[08 - Past perfect]].

# Conectores de secuencia

Son los que dan ritmo narrativo: *First, … Then, … After that, … Suddenly, … A few minutes later, … In the end, / Finally, …*

# Recursos para que enganche

- **Adjetivos y adverbios** de intensidad: *terrified, amazing, quickly, carefully.*
- **Estilo directo** (una frase de diálogo): *"Help!" she shouted.*
- Un **giro** o final con gracia: la historia mejora si pasa algo inesperado.

> [!example]+ Modelo comentado (primera frase dada: "When I opened the door, I couldn't believe my eyes.")
> When I opened the door, I couldn't believe my eyes. *(frase dada)*
> My whole family **was standing** in the living room, and they **had prepared** a huge surprise party just for me! There were colourful balloons everywhere and a big chocolate cake on the table. *(past continuous + past perfect)*
> At first I **was** so shocked that I couldn't say a single word. Then everyone **started** singing, and suddenly I **began** to laugh and cry at the same time. *(secuencia)*
> "Happy birthday!" they all shouted. *(estilo directo)*
> It **was**, without doubt, the best evening of my whole life, and I know I will never forget it. *(cierre)*
> *(≈ 100 palabras — la extensión real del examen)*

> [!warning]+ Trampas ("para pillar")
> - **Cambiar la primera frase**: hay que continuarla, no reescribirla.
> - **Todo en past simple**: mezcla continuous y perfect para subir Language.
> - **Sin conectores de secuencia**: la historia queda plana.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Rellena con el pasado adecuado
> "When I woke up, the sun ______ (shine) and I realised I ______ (oversleep)."

> [!success]- Solución 1
> **was shining** (fondo) / **had overslept** (anterior)

> [!question]+ 2. Ordena los conectores de secuencia
> in the end · first · suddenly · then

> [!success]- Solución 2
> first → then → suddenly → in the end

> [!question]+ 3. Mejora la frase (añade interés)
> "I went out and saw a dog." → ______

> [!success]- Solución 3
> Ejemplo: *"As I was walking out, I suddenly saw a huge, frightened dog running towards me."*
