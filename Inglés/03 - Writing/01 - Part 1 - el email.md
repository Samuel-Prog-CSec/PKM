<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">En la Parte 1 lees un email (normalmente de un amigo) con **cuatro notas** al margen, y tienes que responder cubriendo las cuatro</mark>. Es lo obligatorio, y lo más fácil de puntuar si sigues el molde.

# La estructura fija

1. **Saludo:** *Hi Sam, / Dear Anna,*
2. **Línea de apertura:** *Thanks for your email. / Great to hear from you!*
3. **Cuerpo:** cubre las **4 notas**, una idea por frase, con conectores.
4. **Cierre:** *Write back soon. / See you soon. / Let me know.*
5. **Despedida + nombre:** *Best wishes, / Love, — [tu nombre].*

# El registro: informal

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Como escribes a un amigo, usa tono informal</mark>: contracciones (*I'm, don't*), frases hechas coloquiales (*Guess what!, By the way*), y nada de *Dear Sir*.

# Frases útiles por función

- **Agradecer/reaccionar:** *Thanks for your email. Sorry for the late reply. Great news!*
- **Dar información:** *Actually, … / The thing is, …*
- **Sugerir:** *Why don't we…? How about + -ing? Let's…*
- **Pedir:** *Could you…? Let me know if…*
- **Cerrar:** *Anyway, I'd better go. Write back soon.*

> [!example]+ Modelo comentado (responde a 4 notas: agradecer un plan, decir cuándo puedes, sugerir algo, preguntar)
> Hi Jack,
> Thanks for your email — it was great to hear from you! I'd absolutely love to come to your birthday party, and it's really kind of you to invite me. *(nota 1)*
> Saturday is perfect for me. I'm busy in the morning, but I'm completely free after five, so I could get there early and help you set everything up. *(nota 2)*
> Why don't we play some music and order pizza? Everyone loves that, and it's really easy to organise. We could watch a film later too. *(nota 3)*
> By the way, should I bring anything, or is it all sorted? Just let me know. *(nota 4)*
> Write back soon!
> Love,
> Sam
> *(≈ 100 palabras — la extensión real del examen)*

> [!warning]+ Trampas ("para pillar")
> - **Dejarte una nota sin responder**: relee y marca las 4.
> - **Registro formal** con un amigo: nada de *I am writing to inform you*.
> - **Olvidar saludo/despedida**: cuentan para Organisation.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Ordena el email
> a) Write back soon! b) Hi Tom, c) Love, Alex d) Thanks for your message! e) [cuerpo con las 4 notas]

> [!success]- Solución 1
> b → d → e → a → c

> [!question]+ 2. ¿Formal (F) o informal (I)? Marca las adecuadas para este email
> 1. I am writing to inform you… 2. Guess what! 3. Yours faithfully 4. Why don't we…?

> [!success]- Solución 2
> Informales adecuadas: **2 y 4**. (1 y 3 son formales → no encajan aquí.)

> [!question]+ 3. Reescribe en tono informal
> 1. I would be grateful if you could reply. → ______
> 2. I regret to inform you I cannot attend. → ______

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. *Let me know / Write back soon.* — 2. *Sorry, I can't make it.*
