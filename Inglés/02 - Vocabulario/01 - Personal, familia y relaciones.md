# Personalidad (adjetivos)

`friendly` (simpático), `shy` (tímido), `outgoing` (extrovertido), `hard-working` (trabajador), `lazy` (vago), `honest` (honesto), `generous`, `selfish` (egoísta), `reliable` (de fiar), `stubborn` (cabezota).

# Falsos amigos y cuidado

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Cuidado con estos</mark>: *sympathetic* = comprensivo (no "simpático" → *nice/friendly*); *relatives* = parientes (no "relativos"); *sensible* = sensato (no "sensible" → *sensitive*).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **`get married to`** (no *with*): *She got married **to** Tom.*
> - **`brothers and sisters`** o **`siblings`** para "hermanos" en general (*brothers* solo = varones).
> - *sympathetic* ≠ simpático; *sensible* ≠ sensible.

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Empareja English–Español
> siblings · in-laws · only child · niece · to retire
> (hijo único · sobrina · hermanos · jubilarse · familia política)

> [!success]- Solución 1
> siblings = hermanos · in-laws = familia política · only child = hijo único · niece = sobrina · to retire = jubilarse

> [!question]+ 2. Completa con un verbo del tema
> 1. My grandparents ______ (nacieron) in 1950.
> 2. They ______ (se llevan bien) with their neighbours.
> 3. After 40 years working, she ______ (se jubiló).

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **were born** — 2. **get on well** — 3. **retired**

> [!question]+ 3. Elige el adjetivo de personalidad
> 1. He never lies; he's very ______. (honest / lazy)
> 2. She loves meeting people; she's ______. (shy / outgoing)

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **honest** — 2. **outgoing**

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el falso amigo
> 1. My teacher is very sympathetic and always tells jokes.
> 2. She's a sensible girl; she cries at films.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. …very **friendly/nice**… (sympathetic = comprensivo) — 2. She's a **sensitive** girl… (sensible = sensato)
