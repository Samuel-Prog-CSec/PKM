<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">El cuerpo, la salud y el deporte</mark>: tema con vocabulario muy concreto y colocaciones fijas.

# El cuerpo

`head, face, neck, shoulder, chest, arm, elbow, wrist, hand, finger, leg, knee, ankle, foot/feet, back, stomach`.

# Salud y síntomas

| English | Español |
| --- | --- |
| illness / disease | enfermedad |
| pain / ache | dolor |
| headache / stomachache | dolor de cabeza / de estómago |
| a cold / the flu | resfriado / gripe |
| cough / sneeze | toser / estornudar |
| injury | lesión |
| to hurt | doler / hacerse daño |
| chemist's / pharmacy | farmacia |
| prescription | receta médica |

# Verbos y colocaciones de salud

*I've got **a headache** / **a temperature** / **a sore throat**. My leg **hurts**. She **broke** her arm. I need to **see a doctor** / **make an appointment** / **take medicine**.*

# Deporte y ejercicio

| English | Español |
| --- | --- |
| do exercise | hacer ejercicio |
| go to the gym | ir al gimnasio |
| get fit / keep fit | ponerse / mantenerse en forma |
| team / match | equipo / partido |
| score a goal | marcar un gol |
| win / lose / draw | ganar / perder / empatar |

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Deportes con `do`, `go` o `play`</mark>: *__play__ + con pelota/equipo* (play football, tennis); *__go__ + -ing* (go swimming, running, skiing); *__do__ + otros* (do yoga, do athletics).

# Falsos amigos (para pillar)

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">*constipated* = estreñido (no "constipado" → *have a cold*); *injury* = lesión (no "injuria")</mark>.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **play / go / do** con deportes: *play football, go swimming, do yoga*.
> - **ache vs pain**: *ache* = dolor sordo y persistente, va en compuestos (head-, tooth-, back-, ear-, stomach-); *pain* = dolor más agudo o general (*a sharp pain*, *be in pain*).
> - *constipated* ≠ constipado (¡cuidado!).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. play, go o do
> 1. I ______ swimming twice a week.
> 2. They ______ basketball on Saturdays.
> 3. She ______ yoga every morning.

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **go** — 2. **play** — 3. **do**

> [!question]+ 2. Empareja síntoma–español
> a cold · a headache · a sore throat · to sneeze
> (dolor de garganta · estornudar · resfriado · dolor de cabeza)

> [!success]- Solución 2
> a cold = resfriado · a headache = dolor de cabeza · a sore throat = dolor de garganta · to sneeze = estornudar

> [!question]+ 3. Completa la colocación de salud
> 1. I need to ______ an appointment with the doctor.
> 2. My back really ______.
> 3. He ______ his ankle playing football.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **make** — 2. **hurts** — 3. **broke/hurt**

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el falso amigo
> 1. I can't come to work; I'm constipated (tengo un resfriado).

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. …I**'ve got a cold** (constipated = estreñido)
