<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Una colocación es una combinación de palabras que suena natural en inglés</mark> (*make a mistake*, no *do a mistake*). Dominarlas es lo que distingue un B1 sólido: se puntúan en writing y speaking.

# make vs do (el par que más confunde)

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">`make` = crear/producir; `do` = realizar una tarea o actividad</mark>:

| make | do |
| --- | --- |
| make a decision | do homework |
| make a mistake | do a course |
| make friends | do the shopping |
| make noise | do exercise |
| make an effort | do the housework |
| make progress | do business |

# have, take y get

- **have:** *have breakfast, have a shower, have a rest, have fun, have a good time.*
- **take:** *take a photo, take a break, take a taxi, take an exam, take care, take part in.*
- **get:** *get up, get dressed, get ready, get married, get lost, get better, get on with.*

# Expresiones fijas útiles (writing/speaking)

*in my opinion, on the other hand, as a result, for example, at the moment, in general, by the way, as far as I know, it depends on.* Más conectores en [[02 - Conectores y linkers]].

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **make a mistake / do an exercise** (no al revés).
> - **take an exam** (hacer un examen) vs **pass an exam** (aprobarlo): no confundir *do* con *take/pass*.
> - **have fun / have a good time** (pasarlo bien), no *pass it well*.

# Más colocaciones útiles

- Con **keep**: *keep in touch* (seguir en contacto), *keep calm*, *keep fit*, *keep a promise*.
- Otras muy frecuentes: *pay attention* (prestar atención), *take place* (tener lugar), *give someone a hand* (echar una mano), *make sure* (asegurarse), *have a look* (echar un vistazo).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. make o do
> 1. ______ a decision — 2. ______ your homework — 3. ______ a mistake — 4. ______ the shopping — 5. ______ progress

> [!success]- Solución 1
> 1. **make** — 2. **do** — 3. **make** — 4. **do** — 5. **make**

> [!question]+ 2. have, take o get
> 1. ______ a photo — 2. ______ a shower — 3. ______ married — 4. ______ part in

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **take** — 2. **have** — 3. **get** — 4. **take**

> [!question]+ 3. Completa la expresión fija
> 1. ______ my opinion, it's a good idea.
> 2. It's cheap. ______ the other hand, it's not very good.
> 3. I studied hard and, ______ a result, I passed.

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **In** — 2. **On** — 3. **as**

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el error
> 1. I did a mistake in the exam.
> 2. We had a lot of fun and passed it great.
> 3. She made her homework.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. I **made** a mistake — 2. …**had a great time** — 3. She **did** her homework
