# Lectura y medios

`novel` (novela), `author` (autor), `chapter` (capítulo), `headline` (titular), `article`, `advert` (anuncio), `channel` (canal), `series / episode`.

# Colocaciones

*__be into__ films, __be keen on__ music, __take up__ a hobby, __go to__ a concert, __watch__ a series, __read__ the news.*

<mark style="background: #8000E1A6;">Cuidado con *funny* (gracioso) vs *fun* (divertido/entretenido)</mark>: *a funny film* (te ríes) vs *a fun day* (te lo pasas bien).

# Falsos amigos (para pillar)

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">*argument* = discusión (no "argumento" → *plot*); *lecture* = conferencia (no "lectura" → *reading*); *assist* = ayudar (no "asistir" → *attend*)</mark>.

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **fun vs funny**: *fun* = divertido; *funny* = gracioso.
> - **argument** ≠ argumento (=plot); **lecture** ≠ lectura.
> - **`be into` / `be keen on`** + -ing: *I'm into **playing** chess.*

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Empareja English–Español
> plot · audience · lyrics · headline · band
> (público · titular · argumento · grupo · letra)

> [!success]- Solución 1
> plot = argumento · audience = público · lyrics = letra · headline = titular · band = grupo

> [!question]+ 2. fun o funny
> 1. The party was so ______; I really enjoyed it.
> 2. He told a ______ joke and everyone laughed.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **fun** — 2. **funny**

> [!question]+ 3. Completa con un phrasal (take up / be into / give up)
> 1. I ______ ______ photography last year (empecé).
> 2. She's really ______ ______ jazz (le encanta).

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **took up** — 2. **into**

> [!question]+ 4. Corrige el falso amigo
> 1. The plot was confusing, but the argument between them was funny. (¿quieres decir el argumento de la peli?)
> 2. I went to an interesting lecture (querías decir "lectura").

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. …the **plot** was confusing (argument = discusión) — 2. …an interesting **book/reading** (lecture = conferencia)
