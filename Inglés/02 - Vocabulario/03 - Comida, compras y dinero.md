# Dinero

`cash` (efectivo), `change` (cambio), `coin / note` (moneda / billete), `spend` (gastar), `save` (ahorrar), `afford` (permitirse), `borrow / lend` (pedir prestado / prestar), `owe` (deber).

# Colocaciones

*pay **in cash / by card**, **do** the shopping, **get** a refund, **be on** a budget, it's **good value** for money.*

# Falsos amigos (para pillar)

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">*reasonable* prices = precios razonables; *a receipt* = tique (no "receta" → *recipe*); *to spend* = gastar (dinero o tiempo)</mark>. Ojo: *borrow* = tomar prestado (yo cojo), *lend* = prestar (yo doy).

> [!warning]+ Trampas de examen ("para pillar")
> - **borrow vs lend**: *Can I **borrow** your pen?* / *Can you **lend** me your pen?*
> - **receipt** (tique) ≠ **recipe** (receta).
> - **pay by card** / **pay in cash** (preposiciones fijas).

# Ejercicios

> [!question]+ 1. Empareja English–Español
> receipt · bargain · refund · to afford · to owe
> (ganga · deber · tique · reembolso · permitirse)

> [!success]- Solución 1
> receipt = tique · bargain = ganga · refund = reembolso · to afford = permitirse · to owe = deber

> [!question]+ 2. borrow o lend
> 1. Can I ______ your car this weekend?
> 2. She ______ me €20 yesterday.

> [!success]- Solución 2
> 1. **borrow** — 2. **lent**

> [!question]+ 3. Verbo de cocina
> 1. ______ the water before adding the pasta. (hervir)
> 2. ______ the onions in a little oil. (freír)

> [!success]- Solución 3
> 1. **Boil** — 2. **Fry**

> [!question]+ 4. Completa la colocación
> 1. I paid ______ card. 2. It's good ______ for money. 3. We ______ the shopping on Fridays.

> [!success]- Solución 4
> 1. **by** — 2. **value** — 3. **do**
